About this demo — created by Alix Benoit

Melt pool over time for a laser scanning a Ti-6Al-4V plate along a path you draw, or along one of the 22 simulated test paths in the preset list, which the models never saw during training. The prediction comes from a neural operator (FNO, GAOT or RIGNO), a neural network trained on FLOW-3D WELD simulations run with the validated simulation settings from the paper. Laser power and scan speed can be set anywhere in the range the models were trained on, and frames are spaced by the chosen time step. Predictions are computed on the 75×50×40 grid the models were trained on (the paper reports errors on a finer 150×100×40 grid). Hand-drawn paths that look nothing like the random paths used in training will be less reliable. The error under a comparison is the mean absolute temperature error over the substrate (every cell at or below its surface).
