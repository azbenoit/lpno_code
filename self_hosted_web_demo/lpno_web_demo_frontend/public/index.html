About this demo — created by Alix Benoit

Melt pool of a laser scanning a Ti-6Al-4V plate at power P and scan speed V, averaged over time in a window that moves with the beam. The prediction comes from a neural operator (FNO, GAOT or RIGNO), a neural network trained on FLOW-3D WELD simulations run with the validated simulation settings from the paper. Set P and V with the sliders or by dragging on either map. Tick "Compare to ground truth" to show the 123 simulated cases (96 used for training, 14 for validation, 13 for testing) and click one to compare the prediction with its simulation. The error under a comparison is the mean absolute temperature error over the substrate (every cell at or below its surface).
