About this demo — created by Alix Benoit

Melt pool over time for a laser scanning a Ti-6Al-4V plate along a 0.6 mm straight track at power P and scan speed V. Each of the 20 frames is predicted on its own, from where the beam is and where it has already been, by a neural operator (FNO, GAOT or RIGNO), a neural network trained on FLOW-3D WELD simulations run with the validated simulation settings from the paper. Set P and V with the sliders or by dragging on the map. Tick "Compare to ground truth" to show the 123 simulated cases (96 used for training, 14 for validation, 13 for testing) and click one to see simulation, prediction and their difference side by side. The error under a comparison is the mean absolute temperature error over the substrate (every cell at or below its surface).
